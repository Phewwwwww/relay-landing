Questions, answered

Delegation without giving up control.

Does my receiver need Relay?

No. Relay contacts them over WhatsApp or by phone, so the buyer is the only person who needs the app.

Can Relay act outside my rules?

No. If a transaction falls outside the permissions you set, Relay escalates it to you instead of guessing.

Can I change permissions later?

Yes. Add, remove or restrict trusted receivers at any time from your Relay controls.

Is Relay only for deliveries?

Deliveries are the first use case. The same permission model can later extend to other delegated commerce tasks.
